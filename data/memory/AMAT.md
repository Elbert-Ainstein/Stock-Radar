## Stable Facts

Applied Materials Inc /DE (NASDAQ: AMAT) is the broadest-portfolio wafer-fab-equipment (WFE) vendor, supplying deposition, etch, CMP, RTP, metrology, ion implant tools, and Applied Global Services (recurring revenue). Primary products serve leading-edge logic, DRAM/HBM, and advanced packaging fabs globally. Primary public competitors: ASML (litho monopoly), Lam Research (LRCX, etch/deposition specialist), KLA Corporation (KLAC), Tokyo Electron (TEL). Strategic relationships: TSMC and Samsung EPIC Center partnerships; EssilorLuxottica long-term JDA for next-generation intelligent optical systems (AR/smart glasses). Manufacturing/operations footprint is global; China is a material revenue segment subject to US export-control policy risk. Fiscal year ends late October. D/E ~0.30; net cash ~$973M ($8.24B cash, $7.27B debt) as of 2Q FY26.
