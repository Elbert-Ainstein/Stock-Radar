## Stable Facts

Circle Internet Group, Inc. (CRCL; NYSE) is the issuer of USDC, the #2 stablecoin by circulation and the dominant regulated US dollar stablecoin (~80% of dollar digital-currency transactions). Primary revenue is reserve income: USDC reserves held in cash and short-term Treasuries earn the prevailing yield, making the business a net-interest-margin spread operation. Primary public competitor: Tether (USDT, private/offshore) for total stablecoin share; Coinbase (COIN) for crypto-financial-infra comparables; Stripe/Visa/Mastercard entering as competing rail builders. Strategic relationship: Coinbase receives ~50% of reserve income generated on USDC (contractual distribution split; structural, not transient). Additional products: Circle Payments Network (CPN), Arc blockchain/token platform. Manufacturing footprint: N/A (financial services). Fiscal year ends December 31. IPO: June 2025 at $31/share.
