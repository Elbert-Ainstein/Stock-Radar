## Stable Facts

Lumentum Holdings Inc. (LITE, Nasdaq) designs and manufactures optical and photonic products for cloud/datacenter, telecom, and industrial markets. Primary products: EML laser chips, pump lasers, 800G/1.6T optical transceivers, Optical Circuit Switching (OCS) modules, and Co-Packaged Optics (CPO) components. Primary public competitors: Coherent Corp. (COHR) and Marvell Technology (MRVL); well-funded startups Ayar Labs, Lightmatter, and Celestial AI are emerging CPO rivals. Strategic relationship: NVIDIA — multi-year partnership including multi-billion purchase commitment and $2B investment to fund R&D and a new U.S. fab (announced 2025/2026); NVIDIA identically funded Coherent ($2B) and Marvell ($2B) — deliberate tri-sourcing, non-exclusive. Manufacturing footprint: existing fabs plus NVIDIA-funded new U.S. fab (12–24 month startup timeline). Fiscal year ends late June.
