## Stable Facts

nLIGHT, Inc. (LASR — Nasdaq) is a vertically-integrated high-power laser manufacturer spanning semiconductor chip fabrication through fiber amplification to beam-combined laser systems, pivoting aggressively into directed-energy weapons (DEW). Primary products: high-power fiber lasers, beam-combined laser modules, and the HADES™ family of high-energy laser effectors for land/sea/air/space C-UAS and counter-RAM applications. Primary public competitors: IPG Photonics (industrial fiber lasers), Coherent/Lumentum (photonics); defense prime integrators Lockheed Martin, Northrop Grumman, and Raytheon Technologies are both customers and competitors for HADES weapon-module content. Strategic relationships: sub-system supplier to US DoD primes; expanding Torino, Italy facility for allied/European DEW demand. Manufacturing footprint: US (primary fab) + Torino, Italy (expansion). Fiscal year ends December 31. TTM revenue ~$290M as of Q1 2026; $190M equity raise completed in 2025/early 2026.

Key unknown: will a named multi-year program-of-record materialize within the thesis window, or does the pipeline remain prototype/development through 2027?

Unclear whether primes will in-source beam-combination capability or continue to rely on nLIGHT's vertically-integrated supply — the channel conflict risk is real but unresolved.
