The 50% of net-new on non-seat pricing is encouraging but not yet conclusive proof of the bull case.
2. **Armis integration and cross-sell economics.** Armis was acquired Apr 2026 for ~$7.8B. Cross-sell attach rates, revenue contribution, and margin dilution/accretion timeline are not yet disclosed. Key to whether the M&A is value-creative or dilutive.
3. **Consumption/token pricing model maturity.** 50% of net-new is now non-seat (tokens, infrastructure, connectors). How quickly does this scale, and what are the gross margin implications of frontier-model inference COGS pass-through at scale?
4. **Federal vertical exposure.** Management cited geopolitical prudence in guidance. Degree of federal-vertical concentration and exposure to government IT spending freezes is not fully quantified.

The 20x current level is already below any historical operating range; a "melting ice cube" scenario implies 12–15x, but the distance between 20x and 15x is a further ~25% downside from current levels.
