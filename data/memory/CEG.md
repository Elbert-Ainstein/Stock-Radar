## Stable Facts

Constellation Energy Corp (CEG, NASDAQ) is the largest US nuclear operator and, post-Calpine acquisition (closed Jan 7, 2026), the nation's largest electricity producer. Primary products: nuclear baseload generation, dispatchable natural gas generation, and long-term clean-energy PPAs (Microsoft, Meta, Walmart). Primary public competitors: Vistra (VST), Talen Energy (TLN), NextEra Energy (NEE). Strategic relationships: Microsoft (Crane/TMI PPA ~835MW), Meta, Walmart/Dresden; $1B DOE loan for Crane restart; IRA nuclear PTC floor. Manufacturing/generation footprint: nuclear fleet ~21 GW + Calpine gas fleet ~26 GW; Crane/TMI restart (Pennsylvania) targeting 2027 commercial operation pending FERC interconnection. Fiscal year ends December 31; market values on Adjusted Operating EPS (not GAAP). ~355M diluted shares outstanding post-Calpine.
