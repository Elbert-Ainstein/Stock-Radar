## Stable Facts

Lattice Semiconductor Corp (LSCC, NASDAQ). Pure-play fabless semiconductor company; primary products are small and mid-range low-power FPGAs (Nexus, Avant platforms) plus, pending close, AMI firmware/BMC manageability software for cloud and AI servers. Fiscal year ends approximately late January. US-domiciled; no material China-revenue tail flagged.
