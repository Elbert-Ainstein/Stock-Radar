# The 10× problem — definition & decomposition

**Owner:** Hume · **Defined:** 2026-06-28 · **Status:** problem locked; solution not yet built

The thinking that has to be settled before we build the 10× hunter. This is the *problem*, broken into pieces — not the solution.

---

## Root problem

The goal is to **find 10× opportunities.** The only lens we have is **earnings/valuation**, which answers one question: *"is this fairly priced?"* That's correct for a known, scaling business (1→100) — but a 10× is never produced by fair-pricing a known business. It comes from a **latent capability (fuel) that ignites into a new curve.** That process is invisible to earnings and valuation.

→ **Goal–method mismatch.** The engine measures fair-pricing, the goal is finding ignitable fuel — so it labels real 10× setups "BROKEN" and can't see what creates them.

---

## The six pieces

A 10× hunt must answer **six questions.** Today only the last one exists.

### A — structural: *is there a 10× here?* (two modes — pick by situation)
- **0→1 mode** → fuel / flywheel / new curve
- **1→100 mode** → bottleneck / beneficiary / pricing power

| Piece | What it answers | Produces | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A1 · What are they doing?** | present activity, as fact | the *activity record* (builds / ships / invests in / adopters / partnerships) | **doing, not announcing** — observable only |
| **A2 · Is the fuel real?** | which capability is defensible | tags: real/weak; flywheel/one-shot · *(1→100: bottleneck w/ pricing power vs commodity)* | flywheel = **user count that compounds** (not bought); pricing power = durable chokepoint |
| **A3 · How big is the prize?** | the upside ceiling | class + ceiling: flywheel-0→1 ≈100× · one-shot-0→1 ≈10× · bottleneck-1→100 = value capture · cyclical = bounded | **0→1 = a new curve**, not faster old growth |

### B — tactical: *is it the time, and how do I play it?*
| Piece | What it answers | Produces | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B1 · Ignited? what phase?** | timing in the wave | ignited (y/n/early) + phase + exit trigger | phase is a **current-state fact, not a prediction**; runway = exit |
| **B2 · What's moving now?** | current attention | attention score + direction | **timing only, never thesis** |
| **B3 · Worth it — entry + size?** | value & risk *(exists)* | target, buy-below, conviction, size | never overpay, but set entry **within** the structural+phase stance |

### How they combine
A1→A2→A3 = *"is there a big, real prize?"* · B1→B2 = *"is it igniting, and where's the heat?"* · B3 = *"size the entry inside that."*
**A 10× = real fuel/bottleneck (A) + early ignition with runway & attention (B1/B2) + a sane entry (B3).**

## The data model — what's separate, what's together

**Keep SEPARATE (independent axes — measure each alone).** Test: *can one be high while the other is low?* Yes → separate.
- structural truth ⟂ price · **demand-cycle ⟂ price-cycle** (earnings rising ≠ stock rising — the engine fused these into "late-cycle" and got it wrong) · prize ⟂ timing · fuel ⟂ ignition · attention ⟂ thesis

**Keep TOGETHER (facets of one question):**
- bottleneck + pricing-power + durability · fuel + flywheel · **runway + exit** (the same signal) · value + entry + size

**By level:** sector/chain facts (demand-cycle, bottleneck, phase, capex) computed ONCE and shared; company facts (which name captures it, its position, entry, attention) per name.

**Combination rule = a GATED CASCADE** (not a weighted average; each gate can route out):
1. **Structural gate** — real prize? real fuel (0→1) *or* bottleneck-with-pricing-power (1→100). No → **AVOID**.
2. **Confirmation gate** — is the wheel turning? *Turning is signalled by DEAL FLOW (design wins, customer commitments, capacity reservations, LTAs) — LEADING + factual, not earnings (lagging, already priced).* deals signing / ignition fired / live pricing power → continue · ease only, no deals → **WATCH** · deals drying / pricing power expiring / negative flywheel → **AVOID**.
3. **Runway gate** — room left? (phase: demand runway, bottleneck not about to dissolve). Exhausted → **EXIT/AVOID**.
4. **Value gate** — price sane? (the engine / risk-adj). Fully priced → trim to **WATCH**/small · reasonable → **BUY**.
5. **Size the BUY** — mode (0→1 small/asymmetric · 1→100 larger), correlation (correlated names = one bet), attention (high = market waking → ride · low = accumulate early).

A name reaches BUY only by clearing all four gates; drops to WATCH at the confirmation/value gate; AVOID at structural/runway/reversal. *(Discriminate a binding deal/PO/design-win = "doing" from an MOU/LOI/"intent" = "announcing.")* This is "combine conditionally, not averaged," made explicit. "BROKEN" was the failure of exactly this — every axis collapsed into one word.

## Validation & risk layer — two matrices *(later; needs data we don't have yet)*

- **Factor matrix (axes × axes):** validates the separation — low off-diagonal = clean orthogonal factors; a hot cell = two axes that are secretly the same (collapse one). The real column is each **axis × "did it 10×"** → which pieces earn their keep. *Diagnostic only — NOT the combination engine* (it's linear/pairwise; the real combine is conditional/gated).
- **Company matrix (names × names, within a sector):** confirms the sector is the shared driver (→ the sector/company split), feeds **sizing** (correlated names = one bet — don't double-count), points company analysis at the **residual** (the idiosyncratic edge), and via **lead-lag** gives timing + chain structure. A Type-B risk/timing tool — never a thesis. Both matrices are regime-dependent.

## Principles (the guardrails)
- **Facts over prediction.** Inventory present facts (activity, fuel, bottleneck, capex, attention). Never forecast the market. Fuel & bottleneck are facts you record; ignition you *respond* to, don't predict.
- **Two lenses for two prizes.** Earnings/cash-flow → 1→100 value & entry (B3). Capability / value-chain → the structural prize (A). The engine speaks only the first.
- **Anti-loophole.** Real fuel ≠ slideware (doing not announcing). A flywheel must be *turning* (compounding user count). Pricing power must be *durable* (bottlenecks move: GPU → HBM → packaging → power). Attention is never a thesis.

## Sourcing 0→1 candidates — the vision is the funnel *(resolves "no screen")*

You can't screen for 0→1 (financials miss it) and you can't hand-read 5,000 companies. The intake is the **5–10y vision** (Type A, backward-from-the-future): decide how the world works in 5–10 years → derive the **capabilities it will require** → inventory the companies *already building* those (the fuel) → that's the watchlist. So **Type A does double duty: it sources the candidates *and* sizes the prize.** The vision narrows the universe to a hand-readable set; you only inventory capabilities *within the themes the future requires*.

**Honest residuals:** (1) the whole 0→1 pipeline now rests on the *quality of the vision* — but that's the right place for the hard part (it's where the operator's edge lives); (2) **selection blind spot** — you only find what the vision anticipated, so you'll miss paradigm shifts you didn't foresee (an un-imagined AI-in-2015 winner); (3) the 5–10y vision is the framework's softest, most prediction-like input, and it's now load-bearing — defensible only because *structural* 5–10y shifts are closer to fact than market timing.
