## I. PHILOSOPHY — what the book believes

1. **Compounding is the only game.** 做时间的朋友 — be time's friend. The scoreboard is measured in ages, not deadlines: ~$3–4M band around age 30 at boring rates, eight figures by mid-career, from money already in hand. The deadline was the flaw; the ambition was never the problem.
2. **The edge is runway, not returns.** An 18-year-old with a $40K floor (~8 years of spending) can wait out any cycle. Everything protecting the floor protects the edge.
3. **The honest rate is 10–15%/yr.** Anything promising more must pass the two-sentence test, name its falsifiers, and take starter size regardless.
4. **The Asymmetry Sleeve** (≤25% of book) is where ambition lives legally: 30–40% targets licensed on fat pitches only. High-growth/momentum taste is housed here — never banned, never full-book.
5. **We know where the future leads; we don't know its leaders.** (1999 knew the internet; crowned AOL and Cisco. The real leaders didn't exist yet or traded at $5.) Therefore: own the wave's **physics** while leaders are unknown; run the **fingerprint scan** for leaders revealing themselves; never pay crown prices for wave certainty; survive long enough to meet the winners.
6. **AI turns knowledge into commodity** (the founding screen, user-authored). The question asked of every business: *if intelligence is free, what's left to pay for?* Own what AI can't eat.
7. **Two kinds of company, two rubrics.** 1→n compounders (Track C) and 0→1 category creators (Track Z). Grading a Z on the C rubric kills it falsely; sizing a Z like a C kills the book. Never confuse a 1→n cyclical at parabola with a 0→1 (June 25's true error).

## III. GRADING — Track C (compounders), methodology v1.2.1

**Composite = Σ(weight × score), 0–100 per factor.**
Letters: ≥91.5 **A** · ≥88.5 **A−** · ≥85.5 **B+** · ≥81.5 **B** · ≥78.5 **B−** · ≥75.5 **C+** · ≥71.5 **C** · else **D**.

| Factor | Wt | What it measures | Scoring anchors |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Moat/Durability** | 25% | What's left when intelligence is free. **Every A requires a written one-line answer to that question.** | Unconditional physical/data/license moats top; conditional T2 rails docked; contested-by-rivals docked; monopoly ≠ data moat |
| **Growth** | 20% | Durable multi-year rate + number of engines | 20%+ sustained at scale = A-zone; M&A-flattered growth discounted; contracted backlog (cRPO) quality counts |

| **Profitability** | 15% | GAAP honesty | Widening GAAP losses cap the score regardless of adjusted promises; margin *trajectory* redeems; SBC + serial all-stock dilution penalized |
| **Evidence/Revisions** | 10% | Direction of proof | Guide raises, revisions inflecting up, external validation (paying partners), primary sources only. **v1.1: FY29+ consensus de-weighted — low-N placeholder models; distrust the number, respect the shape** |
| **Momentum-RISK** | 10% | **INVERTED. FOMO hazard, not trend quality** | Extended run = hazard (>+100%/6mo = redline); deep drawdown = hazard drained = high score. This axis is anti-parabola, **never a bottom call**. Re-scored from measured windows, not vibes |
| **Cycle-Risk** | 5% | End-market + regime exposure | Acyclical demand scores high; pre-profit = rate-cycle hostage; policy/reimbursement cycles count; dated supply sunsets (TrendForce-style) hard-cap |

**Grade movement rules:** grades move on **evidence** or on **methodology change** (which triggers same-day backward propagation across ALL standing grades) — never on mood or price alone. Every migration is recorded with cause. Responses can be **pre-registered** (e.g., "named MSFT ITSM bundle ⇒ NOW A−→B+ automatic"). Consistency is enforced: a standard applied to one name applies to every name that day.

## IV. TRACK Z — the 0→1 scorecard (seven fingerprints)

1. **A secret** — the thesis requires believing something competent people reject ("scam" chatter + insider conviction).
2. **Monopoly in miniature** — 60%+ of something small and growing (Amazon-books). Share of a giant existing market = n, not zero.
3. **The 10× axis** — order-of-magnitude better on one dimension, not 20% better.
4. **Non-linear adoption** — usage growing ≫ revenue (monetization hasn't caught the curve).
5. **Unmodelability** — analyst dispersion/absence because no comparable exists (inverted from Track C, where consensus tightness is good).
6. **Founder at the wheel** — painful ownership, decade horizon.
7. **Survival math** — reaches the 1 without dying or diluting away (most 0→1 failures are 0→0 by funding).

**Hunting grounds (swept every Radar):** the IPO orphanage (18–36mo post-IPO trough), the anti-parabola bench treated as a *queue* (real 0→1s always hand you a −40–60% valley entry), category-absence scans, spin-offs/forced sellers.
**Sizing law:** Z-bets live in the Sleeve only, 5–6 starter tickets, staged thirds, power-law expectations (winners pay for zeros — only if there are several tickets). One conviction bet on a story = June 25 wearing a Thiel costume.
**Holding law:** the entire Z return is earned by sitting through the 1→n crossing (years, always including a −50%).

## V. POSITION & RISK LAW — the Charter (operative articles)

- **The floor:** $40,000 is sacred living money. Never invested, never margined, no market outcome can reach it. New cash joins the floor first.
- **Caps:** no new position >25% of book at cost; no single factor >50%. Current book grandfathered — exits by its rules, never by force.
- **Anti-parabola:** anything +100% in trailing 6 months gets starter size only. Conviction is what everyone has at tops.
- **Staged thirds:** every entry. No all-in days, ever again.
- **Euphoria protocol:** any position that doubles fires an automatic consult — trim a preset slice or sign a paragraph defending why not. Thermometer signals: target races, leveraged-ETF launches, itchy hands, euphoric group chats, +70%-in-a-day option wins.
- **Exit architecture at entry:** every position carries a written trigger + never-fills clause (price OR date) + falsifiers. No seat rides naked.
- **Rule 5:** wires and clauses **fire consults, never orders**. No auto-sells. No forced timing. Decisions are signed in daylight, logged forever.
- **De-risking that only executes at good prices isn't de-risking** — real de-risking has an unconditional leg (Rule 11 pattern).
- **Waits are dated bets, not open hopes:** every hold names its next evidence checkpoint. "Hold until fundamentals" decomposes per seat — a no-sunset seat (MU/DRAM) and a dated-sunset seat (SNDK/NAND-2027) are different bets wearing the same word.

## VI. EPISTEMICS — the cognitive system (v0) + data regime

**The five thinking rules:**
1. **Backward propagation** — every new principle re-runs against all standing conclusions the same day it's adopted.
2. **Facts/narrative separation** — sources contribute facts; interpretation is built here; divergence from the source's spin is stated as signal.
3. **Pre-emption** — if the next question is predictable, the deliverable answers it before it's asked.
4. **Contradiction sweep** — before anything ships, check it against everything currently held; contradictions surface as findings.
5. **Original-conclusion requirement** — every analysis contains ≥1 conclusion no source stated and nobody prompted, or it doesn't ship.

**The data regime (each rule = a named scar):**
- **Two-phase verdicts:** same-day closes are PROVISIONAL; wires/clauses adjudicate only on next-day settled prints (INTC $91.63→$92.52; MU $899.85→$900.20).
- **Provenance on every number:** source + timestamp + settled/snapshot flag; two-source cross-check; conflicts flagged, never averaged (the $107.76 ghost).
- **Primary > wraps > aggregators;** aggregator "actuals" are provisional until a filing confirms (TradingKey reported consensus as actuals). User-couriered live prints beat delayed feeds intraday; settled rows beat everything next day.
- **Real clocks, real calendars:** every log stamp from the system clock; market-calendar verified before any "live session" claim (the Korea holiday error; the timestamp drift).
- **Corrections go forward:** the log is append-only; wrong rows get correction rows, prominently, never silent edits. Being wrong is survivable; being wrong quietly is not.
- **Event classification:** PRICE EVENT (flows/narrative; no primary-source fundamental change; protocol = structure holds, no decisions at velocity) vs THESIS EVENT (primary-source confirmed; protocol = immediate re-exam/consult). The tell for price events: the wrong stock falls hardest on the story.

## VII. DECISION ARCHITECTURE — how choices get made

- **Consults with doors:** every fired rule opens a consult ticket with quantified doors (hold-to-checkpoint / staged / unconditional-leg / full), falsifiers named, no recommendation, decision signed by the human, logged with its reasoning.
- **Counterfactual scoreboard kept honestly:** every itch that wasn't acted on gets scored against what discipline did (current: panic-moves 0-for-2, discipline 2-for-0). The record teaches, not the feelings.
- **The seesaw is used against its mood:** hardware⇄software flows oppose short-term while fundamentals link long-term — rotations sell the surging leg and buy the funding leg, never the reverse.
- **EOY rotation plan (standing):** exit AI supply-chain around year-end (evidence-gated review, not auto-exit) → rotate into AI applications, *defined by the screen*: applications that own data, rails, or liability licenses — never knowledge-packagers.
- **The wall:** the separate account is separate in both directions. The book's analysis doesn't flow out as trades; that account's positions don't pull the book's machinery in.
- **The mandate above everything:** this system analyzes; it never advises buy/sell/hold; it never trades. Structure proposes, evidence adjudicates, **the human decides, the log remembers.**
